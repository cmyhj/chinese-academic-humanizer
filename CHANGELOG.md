# Changelog

## 0.3.0

First public release of the current skill.

- Context-first editing: chapter argument chains, paragraph roles, sentence-level decisions, scientific fidelity, and Chinese/English abstract alignment.
- Chinese engineering style guidance informed by two journal papers and one master's thesis, with attributed page anchors.
- Function-based Chinese expression checks rather than word-frequency, sentence-length or anti-detection quotas.
- Separate requirements for plain text, text files and tracked Word delivery.
- Word protection and acceptance/rejection verification requirements; no Word execution engine is bundled.
- Public packaging with local attachment identifiers removed, source acknowledgments, and standard-library validation tests.
